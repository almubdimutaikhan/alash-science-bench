# Rights and redistribution

**Status: not assessed.** No license is asserted for this dataset, which is why the Hub card says
`license: other` / `rights-not-assessed`.

- The `passage` column is a verbatim excerpt (138 to 2,172 characters, median about 600) of OCR text
  from modern collected editions of Alash-era authors. The original works are old, but the editions'
  transcription, commentary and typesetting may carry their own rights. That has not been checked.
- The full OCR books and terminology workbooks are not in this repository and must not be added.
- Questions, answers, distractors, explanations and review records were written for this project by AI
  agents under the maintainer's direction. Who holds rights in agent-written text, and under which
  license it should be released, is a decision for the maintainer.
- Code in `scripts/` has no license file yet.

The GitHub repository was made public on 28 October 2026 at the maintainer's decision, with the excerpt rights
still unresolved. If a rights holder objects, the passages (or the repository) will be removed.

Before this is published on Hugging Face, or promoted as a benchmark: confirm the edition-specific rights for
every cited volume, decide whether to ship excerpts or only offsets and hashes (the dataset still
works without `passage` for the closed-book track), and choose licenses.
